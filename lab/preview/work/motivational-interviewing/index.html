Motivational Interviewing

An e-learning course that teaches support workers the core skills of motivational interviewing.

Learners
Support workers, carers, mental health workers
My part
Learning design, illustration, animation
Built in
Articulate Rise, Illustrator, Vyond

Brief

Teach a counselling approach to people who aren't counsellors: support workers, carers and mental health workers.

Approach

Complex terms broken into simple parts, animated scenes of real conversations, and short quizzes that build confidence.

Result

Preview only: your result line goes here.

I built this e-Learning using Articulate Rise, with imagery personally developed using Adobe Illustrator and animated GIFs produced in Vyond.

Fostering accessibility

This e-Learning caters to professionals seeking to enhance their communication skills and effectively support clients in making positive behavioural changes, ultimately promoting a more client-focused and collaborative approach in their respective fields.

Typically these professionals exist outside of the realm of psychologists and therapists. They may include support workers, carers and mental health workers. In this case, ensuring that complex terms and concepts are broken down into simpler parts is important.

Creating relatable scenarios

Using Vyond to create animated GIFs adds an extra layer of dimension to the e-Learning, bringing character emotions and real-world scenarios to life.

Quizzing

These quizzes serve as effective reinforcement tools, allowing learners to review and reinforce their understanding of key concepts in a non-intimidating manner.

By keeping the quizzes simple and approachable, we strike a balance that encourages engagement and boosts confidence without overwhelming the learner.
