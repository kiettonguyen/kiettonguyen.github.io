Living Museum of the West

A brand and website refresh for a Melbourne ecomuseum, so researchers and first-time visitors both find their way.

Client
Living Museum of the West, Maribyrnong
Team
Five designers
My part
Reusable components; menu, navigation and footer prototypes
Output
Desktop and mobile site, brand refresh

Brief

Refresh the museum's brand and website, desktop and mobile, as a modern take on a community archive.

Two kinds of visitor

Purposeful users who know what they want, and wanderers who came to explore. The structure serves both.

Less scrolling

A grid breaks long pages into blocks, each with an image, so nobody scrolls forever.
