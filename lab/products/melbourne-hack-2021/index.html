AnyExercise, Melbourne Hack 2021

A web app that makes home exercise easy and rewarding for older people, with pose detection to check their form.

Event
Melbourne Hack 2021: 1st place and the Arcitecta "Greatest Maker" prize
Role
Design lead: wireframes, prototype, brand, interactions
Project
AnyExercise on Devpost ↗

Problem

Lockdowns closed gyms and parks, and many older people stopped moving, which raises the risk of falls.

Idea

Borrow from Ring Fit and Wii Fit: trackers, progress and small wins that make exercise feel like a game.

Design

Big, calm, uncluttered screens that older users can read at a glance.
