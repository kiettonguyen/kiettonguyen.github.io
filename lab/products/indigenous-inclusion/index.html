Indigenous Inclusion

A story-led e-learning module on the history, culture and experience of Aboriginal Australians.

Type
Self-paced e-learning
Role
Learning design, illustration, motion
Built in
Articulate Storyline
With
An Aboriginal Australian subject matter expert

Brief

Turn an old slide deck into e-learning that people with no background knowledge actually finish.

Approach

Story first. Animated scenes, including an isometric map of land, water and sky, carry the history.

Care

Every page was shaped with an Aboriginal subject matter expert, for accurate and respectful representation.
