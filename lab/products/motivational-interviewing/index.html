Motivational Interviewing

An e-learning course that teaches support workers the core skills of motivational interviewing.

Learners
Support workers, carers, mental health workers
Role
Learning design, illustration, animation
Built in
Articulate Rise, Illustrator, Vyond

Brief

Teach a counselling technique to people who aren't counsellors.

Approach

Plain words, one idea per block. Animated scenes show the conversations, so learners see the skill before they name it.

Checks

Short, low-stakes quizzes that build confidence rather than catch people out.
